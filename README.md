# 📌 Vrinda Store Annual Report 2022

## 🎯 Project Goal
To analyze the yearly sales performance of Vrinda Store and identify customer segments, top-performing states, sales channels, and monthly trends to help the business improve revenue and make data-driven decisions.

 ⭐ Key Insights & Findings
- Women customers contributed more sales than men
- Adult age group recorded the highest number of purchases
- Maharashtra, Karnataka and Uttar Pradesh were the top revenue-generating states
- Amazon and Flipkart were the primary sales channels with maximum orders
- Festival season months and year-end showed peak orders
- Majority of sales came from clothing & apparels category
- Repeat customers contributed significantly to the total revenue



🔥 Key Features of the Report
- Clean & transformed dataset for analysis
- Interactive dashboard with slicers and filters
- Gender-wise, age-wise and state-wise performance comparison
- Top 5 revenue-generating products and categories
- Monthly sales trend & seasonality analysis
- Sales channel breakdown and customer segmentation

 📊 Metrics Used
- Total Sales
- Total Quantity Sold
- Total Orders
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Revenue by State
- Revenue by Gender
- Revenue by Age Group
- Revenue by Platform
